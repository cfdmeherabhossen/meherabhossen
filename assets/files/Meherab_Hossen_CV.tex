\section*{Research Profile}
I am a Mechanical Engineering graduate and CFD Simulation Engineer working on multiphase flow,
heat transfer, and phase change. I develop and validate numerical models in ANSYS Fluent and
OpenFOAM against published benchmarks, with research in thermal energy storage and droplet dynamics.
