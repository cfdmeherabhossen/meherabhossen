# Project figure sources

Figures are rendered directly from the corresponding supplied PDF reports:

- gear-deformation.png: gear-train.pdf, physical page 13, first figure (total deformation).
- belt-cad.png: belt-drive.pdf, physical page 12, final CAD model.
- labview-diagram.png: labview-mechatronics.pdf, physical page 17, block diagram.
- solar-generation.png: solar-pv-net-metering.pdf, physical page 29, generation profile.

The two poster JPEGs in the parent image folder are frames at 12 seconds from
meherab.MP4 (droplet pair) and meherab1.mp4 (falling droplet). The original recordings
and all report PDFs remain available in their existing locations.

Droplet impact contour PNGs are unmodified copies of the owner-supplied frames
0000, 0028, 0056 and 0084 from droplet_impact.0000.png–droplet_impact.0084.png.
They show water volume fraction and are not labelled as freezing results.

Alnakeeb media update: sl-v.png is the new owner-supplied liquid-fraction
comparison (Alnakeeb, ANSYS Fluent and OpenFOAM). sl_opi_1.png is an unmodified
copy of montage_preview_1.png; montage_preview.png has the identical SHA-256
and is not displayed a second time. The montage retains all times and scales.

Alnakeeb animation: assets/videos/sl_opi_1.mp4 is a web-optimized copy of the
owner-supplied PCM_melting_10s.mp4, retaining the same frames, timing and
1920×1080 resolution. alnakeeb-melting-poster.jpg is the frame at 3 seconds from
that animation, extracted without cropping or changing the simulation content.
The owner confirmed that he created the animation; its website caption credits
Md. Meherab Hossen. The original source MP4 is preserved on the owner's computer;
the website copy retains the supplied content and embedded text.


## particle-settling-pac05.png

Plot generated from the owner-supplied 0.5% PAC comparison data. Present-study values: Md. Meherab Hossen, ANSYS Fluent (confirmed by the owner). Reference CFD and experimental comparison values: Awad et al., *Journal of Petroleum Science and Engineering* 199 (2021), 108326, Figure 6, https://doi.org/10.1016/j.petrol.2020.108326. The reference article is published under CC BY. The plot covers Cross and Carreau models at particle diameters 2, 3, 4 and 5 mm.
